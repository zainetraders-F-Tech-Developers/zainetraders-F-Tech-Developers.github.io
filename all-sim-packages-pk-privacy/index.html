Overview

All SIM Packages PK is an offline information app that shows mobile package details (prices, data, minutes, SMS, validity and subscription codes) for Pakistani networks. The package information is built into the app and was collected from publicly available official sources.

Data we collect

The app itself does NOT collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. There is no account, no login and no sign-up. Favourites, saved packages and reminder settings are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and share the following data types for advertising purposes, as described in Google's policies: approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or other IDs. Advertising ID use can be controlled or reset in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed for its features: notifications (for package expiry reminders, only if you save a package and enable reminders) and internet access (used only for serving ads). Dial actions always open your phone's dialer — the app never makes calls itself.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (advertising): https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Accuracy disclaimer

This app is not affiliated with Jazz, Zong, Ufone, Telenor, SCO/SCOM, PTCL or Onic. Package prices and offers change frequently; details were last updated on 14 November 2026. Please confirm a package's status code or your network's helpline before subscribing.
