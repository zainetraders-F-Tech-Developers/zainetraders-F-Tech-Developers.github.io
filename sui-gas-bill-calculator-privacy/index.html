Privacy Policy — Sui Gas Bill Calculator

Last updated: 14 November 2026

"Sui Gas Bill Calculator" is an offline utility app developed by F-Tech Developers. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information the app collects

The app itself does NOT collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

  • All calculations are performed entirely on your device.
  • Your calculation history, saved settings and edited gas rates are stored only in the app's local storage on your own device. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party, and they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.
  • The app does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, gas consumer / reference number, or any account login.
  • The app does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner advertisement provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's Advertising ID,
  • general device information (such as model and operating-system version),
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from the IP address,
  • ad interaction data.

This data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app requests only the permissions needed for this: Internet access and network-state access, used solely for loading the banner ad. All calculator features work fully offline.

Third-party services

Apart from Google AdMob (described above), the app uses no third-party services, no cloud storage and no external databases.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience of adults who manage household utility bills. It does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Estimates disclaimer

The app provides bill estimates based on published OGRA tariff values, which the user can edit inside the app. Estimates may differ from an actual SNGPL or SSGC bill. The app is not affiliated with SNGPL, SSGC or OGRA.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
